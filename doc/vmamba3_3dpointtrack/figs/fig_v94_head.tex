\node[lbl] at (0,-7.0)
  {\textbf{v94: the same evidence, a different function of it.} Both paths read one pair of
   flow vectors per point per frame and nothing else --- no depth, no appearance, no refiner
   feature. The upper path is the front-end's forward--backward test: it reduces the pair to
   one residual, compares it against a fixed tolerance, and then \emph{latches}, so
   $v_{t+1}=v_t\wedge ok_t$ can only ever fall. The lower path hands the two vectors to a
   network instead. Two consequences follow. It can learn a tolerance that depends on flow
   magnitude rather than a constant $\alpha,\beta$; and, because it decides each frame from its
   own evidence (from past frames only, or from the whole clip --- both are trained and both are
   reported), it can mark a point visible again after an occlusion --- which the mask cannot
   do at all, at any threshold. That second point is not a refinement but a repair: $63\%$,
   $52\%$ and $66\%$ of adt, drivetrack and pstudio points re-appear in ground truth, and the
   mask produces exactly zero re-appearances on all three.};
