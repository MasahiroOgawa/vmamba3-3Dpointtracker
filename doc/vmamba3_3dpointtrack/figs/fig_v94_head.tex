\node[lbl] at (0,-7.6)
  {\textbf{Both paths read the same two flow vectors and nothing else} --- no depth, no
   appearance, nothing from the depth refiner. \emph{forward--backward check}: follow the point
   one frame forward by $f_t$ and back again by $b_{t+1}$; if it misses its start by more than
   $\alpha(\lVert f_t\rVert + \lVert b_{t+1}\rVert) + \beta$, call it occluded. The tolerance is a
   constant, identical for a point drifting slowly and one crossing the frame.
   \emph{logical AND with previous}: $v_{t+1} = v_t \wedge ok_t$, combining this frame's check
   with the previous frame's answer, so the check's result is
   carried forward and a single failure fixes the point as occluded for every later frame ---
   which is why the middle bar in the sketch never restarts, at any threshold. It is the one step
   the lower path has no counterpart for.
   \emph{MLP $8\to64$}: eight scalars per point per frame, all of them functions of $f_t$ and
   $b_t$ --- the two vectors, their lengths, and the two forward--backward errors --- embedded to
   $64$ dimensions. \emph{Mamba-3 over frames}: two layers mixing along the time axis only, never
   across points, so each point is judged on its own track. A linear head and a sigmoid follow.
   The outputs differ in kind: the rule emits one bit, the network a probability, read as visible
   above $0.5$. $63\%$, $52\%$ and $66\%$ of adt, drivetrack and pstudio points reappear after
   being occluded, and the latched rule recovers none of them.};
